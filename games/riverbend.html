Riverbend

Dig streams downhill from mountain springs and build watermills to catch the splitting flow.

  • 2-4 players
  • Designed by Jayadev Haddadi
  • Coming to Board Game Arena
  • Free print-and-play coming soon

The game

Melting snow feeds the springs high in the valley. Players dig streams downhill and build watermills along the banks. Water only flows down or level, never uphill or in loops. Every time a stream splits, its flow is shared out, so each new branch weakens the water further down.

Your rivals' streams can steal water from your mills, but they can also feed them, so every dig helps or hurts someone.

How a turn works

  • Take 2 actions of any kind, in any order and any mix.
  • Dig a stream channel; water rushes in at once.
  • Place or move one of your two watermills onto a watered cell.

Scoring

  • At the end of every round, each mill scores the water flowing past it.
  • A mill on a dead-end pond, where a stream stops, scores 3 extra.
  • The game lasts 6 rounds with 2 or 3 players and 5 rounds with 4 players.

The Meadow map

The valley is a green Meadow of springs and channels laid out on a grid. The board size grows with the number of players, and the springs are set up fresh each game, so no two valleys are the same.
